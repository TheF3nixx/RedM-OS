SECTION I: OVERVIEW
The Reanimated Tactical Infantry Unit (RTIU) program—codenamed "Project Fallen Light"—is a classified 
military initiative focused on the recovery, stabilization, and conditioning of deceased combatants for 
reactivation in hostile scenarios. These units, colloquially known as Soldier-Class Zs, represent a new 
tier of biological warfare agents that exhibit obedience, regeneration capacity, and adaptive memory reflexes 
under tightly controlled environments.

Contrary to public knowledge, RTIUs are not mindless undead. They are neurologically synchronized shells, 
preserved via synthetic neuromodulation and reprogrammed using a proprietary signal matrix interface (SMI).

Note: All mentions of “control frameworks” and “base protocol injectors” must be redacted in field briefings.

SECTION II: PRE-CONDITIONING
Prior to neuroreanimation, each subject undergoes the following stages:

Cellular stasis optimization (CSO): Cryo-lock of key tissues and select neural clusters.
Memory thread excision (MTE): Removal of all emotional and identity-linked engrams.
Obedience grafting: Integration of a behavior module using encrypted harmonic pulses at 144.6 Hz, targeting 
decayed synaptic receptors.

Surprisingly, the parietal cortex appears crucial to post-mortem behavioral patterns, even when other 
regions are necrotic. Experiments show higher aggression and coordination when this region remains intact.

SECTION III: CONDITIONING CYCLE
RTIUs undergo a Conditioning Cycle (CC) lasting 9–12 hours:

Stimulus bombardment (visual/auditory cues, false battlefield loops)
Pain-resilience flooding (synthetic overcharge to nociceptor networks)
Reflex memory imprinting (combat drills via closed-loop sim injection)
Language and directive reduction ("Yes/No/Target/Obey" vocabulary grafted via mnemonic bursts)
Emotional bleaching (subject exhibits zero hesitation or empathy)

Once stabilized, units are sealed within containment vaults and tagged via subdermal index glyphs. 
These tags are non-numerical, often symbolic or derived from a language unknown to field staff (see Addendum G-04).

Example tag: LOPYAJ-K’LASP-FEWIRM — suspected to be a mnemonic anchor or trigger phrase.

SECTION IV: KNOWN FAILURES
Despite a 72% deployment success rate, recurring anomalies persist:

Phantom recall: Subjects halting mid-operation, muttering non-verbal sequences.
Visual bleeding: Hemorrhaging from ocular sockets during synchronization drift.
Aggression loop stalls: Subjects targeting "non-existent enemies", or mimicking human voices with no prompt.

The pattern of these failures suggests an external resonance interfering with command protocols. 
Investigations into the SMI interference remain inconclusive. Several engineers have raised concerns 
about the "origin" of the signal matrix framework used, but no documentation exists identifying its original source.
