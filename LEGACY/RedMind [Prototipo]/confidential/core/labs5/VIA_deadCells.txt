I. INTRODUCTION
This document outlines the confirmed occurrence of active cognitive discharge in a deceased
biological subject, designated as Delta-102, during a recovery protocol following Event HFM-029. 
The phenomenon, known as DeadCells, describes an unexpected persistence of structured neurological 
activity after certified biological death, distinct from typical post-mortem neural decay.

II. BACKGROUND
Subject Delta-102 was recovered at Site V-12 during Phase 3 of data excavation. 
Biometric status was confirmed non-viable upon retrieval: core temperature at 23.1 °C, 
flat EEG and ECG readings, cellular decomposition beginning at minor tissue levels (mouth, cornea, fingertips).

At T+13m following retrieval, monitoring equipment registered anomalous microelectrical 
emissions from the cranial region. These emissions were structured, following linguistic 
and rhythmic patterns. Signal intensity escalated and was recorded in full under the file bleed.mem.

The DeadCells phenomenon represents an active post-mortem residual intelligence, 
distinct from hallucinations, echo memory, or standard neural rebound. Delta-102 
displayed coherent vocalizations, syntactic structure, and emotional expressivity 
in the absence of pulse, respiration, or metabolic activity.

This confirms the existence of neuropathic latency fields that retain partial self-awareness 
beyond death, triggered under unknown conditions.
